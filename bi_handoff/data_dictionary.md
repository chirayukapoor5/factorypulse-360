# FactoryPulse 360 — Enterprise Data Dictionary

## 1. Overview & Provenance Disclosure
This data dictionary details the tables, columns, physical data types, constraints, descriptions, and data lineage for FactoryPulse 360.
- **Internal Manufacturing Telemetry**: Generated by the deterministic discrete-event factory simulator modeling 3 production lines, 6 manufacturing cells, and 24 machines.
- **External Predictive Maintenance Telemetry**: Curated from the authentic **UCI AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset** (Dua & Graff 2019 / Matzka 2020). The source data is preserved unmodified; 5 continuous numeric sensors are mapped into `FactSensor` with `is_external = 1` and deterministic synthetic machine/date assignments.
